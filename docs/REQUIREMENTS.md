# Requirements Baseline

## Business outcomes
- Convert mechanic brand awareness into genuine recommendation and stocking behaviour.
- Support non-stockist advocates without forcing a stocking commitment.
- Prioritise useful tools and equipment access.
- Provide professional visibility, training and recognition.
- Scale across India's fragmented mechanic ecosystem.
- Measure programme outcomes and expenditure transparently.

## Primary roles
1. Mechanic
2. Puncture shop owner
3. Field representative
4. Dealer / distributor
5. Eurogrip administrator

## Core capability groups
- Identity and access management
- Mechanic CRM and professional passport
- Advocacy, fitment and stocking ledger
- Tool Bank and rewards
- Product catalogue and fitment verification
- TrustVision evidence review
- Document-grounded AI Copilot
- Specialised agent workflows with approval gates
- Training and certification
- Geospatial coverage and territory planning
- Notifications and integrations
- Fraud review and audit
- Executive analytics and ROI

## Non-functional requirements
- Secure, role-scoped access.
- Persistent database-backed records.
- API documentation and versioning.
- Offline-tolerant workflows where appropriate.
- Input validation, structured errors and audit trails.
- Automated tests.
- No fabricated metrics, inventory, prices, deliveries or AI accuracy.
- Human approval for expensive equipment, financial rewards, permission changes and irreversible actions.
